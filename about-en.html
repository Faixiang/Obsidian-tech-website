Reliable connectivity for every device

Founded in 2016, OBSIDIAN Technologies focuses on industrial and residential IoT. We do one thing: keep devices working reliably in real environments, and keep the complexity on our side.

VISION

Vision

Reliable connectivity for every device. We don't chase unit counts — we make sure delivered devices are still stable on day 1,000.

MISSION

Mission

Keep data collection and edge decision-making on the factory floor plain enough that engineers can deploy and maintain it without understanding complex protocols.

VALUES

Values

Describe limits honestly · Use data instead of adjectives · Traceability over looks · Still verifiable after delivery.

01

Milestones

Started with a single sensor terminal and gradually completed the three stages: collection, connectivity and energy management.

2016

Company founded

Established at Hong Kong Science Park with 8 engineers, focused on low-power design for industrial sensor terminals.

2019

First terminal S1 in mass production

First-generation environmental sensors shipped for air quality monitoring at three manufacturing sites.

2022

Edge gateway G2 and energy kit E3

Added local edge processing and energy management, completing three full product lines.

2026

Devices online pass 50,000

Covering Hong Kong, Macau and Southeast Asia across 4 countries and regions, with 99.9% connectivity over the last 12 months.

02

Where we work

The same system, deployed differently at three densities.

FACTORY

Factory

S1 units are deployed zone by zone on the shop floor, with each G2 managing 8 terminals. Local decisions keep working when the factory network stutters.

BUILDING

Building

E3 splits circuits by floor and use, so property managers can allocate cost by actual bills rather than estimated floor area.

HOME

Residential

A single wall-mounted S1 reports on its own — no gateway or home router required.
